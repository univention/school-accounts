This section lists the changes to the School Accounts app.

.. _release-1-0-0:

1.0.0 (2026-10-01)
==================

* First release of School Accounts.
  School administrators and teachers open it from the portal
  and look after the user accounts of their school in the web browser.
* Search the users of a school
  and narrow the list down by school class and work group.
* Reset the password of one or more users at once.
  A confirmation dialog shows who is affected before anything changes.
  Teachers reset the passwords of the students in their classes,
  school administrators those of the other users of their school.
* The user interface is available in English and German.
* The app requires the Guardian and the Kelvin REST API in version 4.1.0 or later
  on the same system, and Keycloak in the domain.
